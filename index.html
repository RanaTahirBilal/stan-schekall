About Stan

Hardware, software and the space between

Stan Schekall has built his career on combining traditional electronics with advanced computing. Since 2016 he has led GF Hi-Tech, a Silicon Valley product development company, as Chief Executive Officer and Engineering Project Lead.

His days are hands-on: talking with clients about their projects, managing work to deadlines, writing software and designing circuit boards. His recent work focuses on where artificial intelligence meets conventional electronics, as computers take on more of the job of controlling electronic systems.

Before GF Hi-Tech he held engineering roles at Lockheed Missiles and Space, Seagate Technology, Intrinsix, Qualcomm, Nano Integrated Solutions and Evans Analytical Group.

Notable work

Problems he has solved

Jasper Display · around 2020

HDMI video on a single FPGA

A compact system that handled HDMI video processing on one field-programmable gate array, giving real-time image processing through a single HDMI cable to a display. Built on flexible Verilog code with an ARM processor, in a device about the size of a deck of cards. The work contributed to the company's acquisition.

Redwire · current consulting

An AI-capable microsatellite

Consulting on a government spacecraft microsatellite project with Redwire, a company under contract to NASA. His part is designing the systems that let the satellite operate with artificial intelligence, so that it can manage many of its own functions in orbit.

Method Mobility · the Wheelchair Project

Steering by leaning

Helped design a wheelchair that users steer and move simply by leaning, intended for people with limited hand movement, including those with paraplegia.

Seagate Technology · 1995

A patent in disk drive reliability

As an ASIC engineer at Seagate he earned a US patent for correcting header information in magnetic disk drives, an early sign of a long interest in making electronic systems more reliable.

GF Hi-Tech, Inc.

A Silicon Valley design practice

GF Hi-Tech provides circuit design, PCB layout, embedded design and FPGA development, from prototype to production.

PCB design

System architecture, analog, digital and mixed-signal circuits, high-speed and high-density layout, design for EMC.

Embedded

AVR, ARM and Xilinx Zynq hardware; Linux device drivers and bare-metal firmware; motion control and battery management.

FPGA

IP core design, simulation, hardware verification, debugging and timing closure.
